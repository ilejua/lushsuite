Set up Lushsuite on your own account

Lushsuite turns a plain-English brief into a list of UK or UAE finance decision-makers, reveals their work emails, writes a different opening line for each one, and sends them from your mailbox. About five minutes to get running.

It runs entirely on your accounts

Your Lusha seat and credits do the searching. Your Gmail sends the emails and gets the replies. Nothing routes through anyone else's account — which is also why you can't just open someone else's copy and start using it.

Get the file

One HTML file — the whole tool.

Connect Lusha and Gmail to your Claude account

On claude.ai go to Settings → Connectors and add both. The tool looks for them under exactly the names Lusha and Gmail — if yours are named differently, rename them to match.

3

Publish it as your own page

Put index.html in a folder on its own, open that folder in Claude Code or the Claude desktop app, and paste this:

Publish index.html as an artifact. It calls my Lusha and Gmail connectors — declare Lusha with prospecting_contact_search, prospecting_contact_enrich, prospecting_contact_filters, prospecting_company_filters and account_usage, and Gmail with create_draft and send_message. Also declare sample.

Claude publishes it and gives you a URL. Open it, allow Lusha and Gmail when asked, and your credit balance should appear in the top right. That's it working.

Don't paste the file into an ordinary Claude chat

An artifact made inside a conversation gets a simpler version of the connector interface, and none of the calls this page makes exist there — it will load and then do nothing. It has to be published from Claude Code or the desktop app.

Why you can't just use someone else's link

A page that calls connectors can never be opened from a public link, on any plan. On Pro there is no other kind of link, so everyone runs their own copy. That is also the safe answer: nobody can spend your Lusha credits or send mail as you from a link somebody forwarded.

Before you send anything

  • Send from the address you want replies on. A personal Gmail is poor for deliverability and wrong for client contact — connect your work account.
  • Create drafts first. The default button writes one draft per recipient into your Gmail so you can read a few before anything leaves. "Send now" is deliberately gated behind typing the recipient count.
  • Press "Personalise each email" before sending. Without it every recipient gets the same text with a name swapped in, which is exactly what makes cold email fail.
  • Cold outreach for FX and trade finance is a financial promotion. It needs the same internal sign-off as anything else you would send a prospect. The tool will not get that for you.
  • Fill in the signature field. It carries your name and the opt-out line and is appended to every message. Under UK GDPR you have to identify yourself and honour objections.
  • Gmail caps sending at roughly 500 a day on a personal account and 2,000 on Workspace. A sudden spike from a cold domain is what trips spam filtering — build up gradually.

One thing your copy won't know

Lushsuite remembers everyone it has shown you and everyone you have emailed, so the same person never comes back twice. That memory lives in your browser, on your copy alone.

If two of you work the same sector, neither copy knows what the other has sent, and you can both approach the same finance director. Split the sectors between you, or ask for the shared version — that needs a proper backend rather than a page.
