Setting up Lushsuite on your own account

Lushsuite turns a plain-English brief into a list of UK or UAE finance decision-makers, reveals their work emails, drafts the approach and sends one personalised email each. This page is how you get your own copy running.

What you need first

Claude Pro, Max, Team or EnterpriseArtifacts aren't available on the free plan
A Lusha seatSearches spend your own credits
GmailApproaches send from your mailbox
Claude Code or the Claude desktop appSigned in to your Claude account
Everything runs on your account, not the sender's

Lushsuite calls Lusha and Gmail as you. Your credits get spent, your mailbox sends, replies come back to your inbox. Nobody can send mail as you from a link, and you can't use someone else's Lusha seat.

Can I just open a link someone sent me?

Only if your organisation is on a Team or Enterprise plan. A page that calls connectors can never be opened from a public link, on any plan — so on Pro and Max there is no link to send, and each person publishes their own copy instead.

Their planCan they send you a working link?
Team / EnterpriseYes — shared inside the organisation, with named people or everyone in it. An Owner also needs Enable artifact connectors switched on.
Pro / MaxNo — the page stays private to whoever built it. Use the steps below.

Publishing your own copy

Takes about five minutes. You get a private page on your own connectors and your own credits.

Step 1
Get the file

Ask for index.html from the Lushsuite repository, or have it sent to you directly. Save it in a folder on its own.

Step 2
Connect Lusha and Gmail

On claude.ai, go to Settings → Connectors and add both Lusha and Gmail. The page looks for them under exactly those names — if yours are named differently, rename them to match.

Step 3
Publish it

Open the folder in Claude Code or the Claude desktop app, signed in to your Claude account, and paste this:

Publish index.html as an artifact. It calls my Lusha and Gmail
connectors — declare Lusha with prospecting_contact_search,
prospecting_contact_enrich, prospecting_contact_filters,
prospecting_company_filters and account_usage, and Gmail with
create_draft and send_message. Also declare sample.

Claude publishes the page and gives you a URL. Open it, allow Lusha and Gmail when asked, and the credit counter in the top right should fill in. That's it working.

Don't paste the file into a normal Claude chat

An artifact made inside a conversation runs a different, simpler version of the connector interface — none of the calls this page makes exist there, so it will load and then do nothing. It has to be published from Claude Code or the desktop app.

Before you send anything

  • Send from the address you want replies on. A personal Gmail is poor for deliverability and wrong for client contact; connect your work account.
  • Create drafts first. The page's default button writes one draft per recipient into your Gmail so you can read a few before anything leaves. "Send now" is deliberately gated behind typing the recipient count.
  • Cold outreach for FX and trade finance is a financial promotion. It needs the same internal sign-off as anything else you'd send a prospect. The tool won't get that for you.
  • Fill in the signature field. It carries your name and the opt-out line, and it's appended to every message. Under UK GDPR you have to identify yourself and honour objections.
  • Gmail caps sending at roughly 500 a day on a personal account and 2,000 on Workspace. A sudden spike from a cold domain is what trips spam filtering — build up gradually.

One thing your copy won't know

Lushsuite remembers everyone it has shown you and everyone you've emailed, so the same person never comes back twice. That memory lives in your browser, on your copy alone.

If two of you work the same sector, neither copy knows what the other has sent, and you can both approach the same CFO. Agree who covers what, or ask for the shared version — that needs a proper backend rather than a page, and it's worth building once four or more people are using this.
